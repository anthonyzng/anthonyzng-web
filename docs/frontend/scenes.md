# Frontend: two worlds, the contact screen, tags, cats and dock

Moved from `CLAUDE.md` section 6.1.

- **Two worlds, the contact screen, tags, cats and dock** (designed with the owner in an interactive prototype): each theme is its own scene. Canvas drawing is the one exception to "animate only transform/opacity"; every canvas is sized by `canvasScale` (`resolution.ts`): the device's density capped per scene and held within a pixel budget per kind (`CANVAS_BUDGET` in `motion.ts`: the black hole's shader and the backdrop about 2.1 MP, just over a 1080p screen, the hero's ink 2.4 MP, the contact screens 3 MP but never under one device pixel per CSS pixel, since the ink writes words), so a 4K screen (or a large window at a high density) draws these soft scenes at a lower resolution and the browser scales them up: measured at 2560×1440 CSS pixels and density 1.5, the canvases went from 24 MP (dark) and 35 MP (light) to 8 and 13, the shader from 8 MP a frame to 2 (owner report: the page stuttered on a 4K screen with 8 GB of memory). The night sky redraws its drifting stars at `SKY_IDLE_FPS` (30) and only a shooting star at the full rate, and the page glass blurs with a smaller radius on very wide screens (≥ 100rem), since a backdrop blur costs its area times its radius on every frame the backdrop moves. Every scene runs on gsap's ticker only while it shows (an IntersectionObserver per scene, paused in a hidden tab), on the phone budget (`budget.ts`: below md or a coarse pointer) where phones need it, and draws one still frame under reduced motion; without a 2D or WebGL context its canvas simply stays empty. **Dark**: the hero is the night around a black hole (`useBlackHole` + `blackHole.ts`: one WebGL quad, its shader in units of the shadow radius: a lensed starfield, dust spiralling in, dark rings sliding in over the horizon with a slow breath, the photon ring and the lensed far side of the disk, the tilted disk whose near half passes in front, Doppler-brightened; drawn at the device's resolution (capped at 1.5, 1.25 on the phone budget, which also takes one noise octave less) and stepped down when frames run long, never below `HERO_SCENE.minScale`); moving the pointer (or touching) throws meteors that curve in under gravity and flash the ring as they cross it (at most `HERO_SCENE.meteors`; coarse-pointer devices also get one now and then). Without WebGL, a still CSS glow (`.hero-void-fallback`). Behind every other section the sky (`useNightSky`, `PageBackdrop`): three slow nebula glows (CSS transforms) and stars streaming along a turning current, paused while the opaque hero fills the screen; whenever a section reaches the middle of the screen (`sectionArrival.ts`) a shooting star crosses left to right at that section's own angle (`BACKDROP.shootAngles`). The contact screen (`sections/Closing.tsx`, `id="contact"`, `data-closing`, one screen tall, no numbered shell: the chapters are Experience, Projects, Skills and Tools, `NUMBERED_SECTION_IDS`, "01 / 04") puts each channel (and the CV once uploaded) in the sky as a meteor of its own colour (`useMeteorContact`: drifting slowly enough to click and stopping while pointed at, growing and shrinking, kept `CONTACT_SCENE.spacing` apart); in turn one swings in beside "Contact me" (`home.closing.title`, `tabindex=-1`, the landing focus), at its largest exactly on the title's line, the title takes its colour and, while it does, is that channel's link (`home.closing.titleLink`); clicking a meteor opens its channel. The bar at the foot is the accessible way in: the channels as real links, the location (screen-reader text) and "Send a message". **Light**: ink on paper. One bamboo grove stands down both edges of the page, fixed behind every section (`useBambooGrove`: seeded stalks, `stalkSpecs`, each painted once to its own canvas, leaves turned outwards from the text; the light hero is see-through, so it is the same grove from top to bottom); each arriving section sets it swaying once about its roots, and now and then a leaf drifts down; between those nothing is redrawn. The hero paints hills in mist and a stream between dry-brush banks (`useInkStream`, painted once, its foot fading into the page, `.hero-stream`), with crescent ripples carried downstream; moving the pointer shakes leaves loose (at most `HERO_SCENE.leaves`), which flutter to the stream and float away. The paper is a faint grain (`paperTile`, `inkPainting.ts` holds the shared ink drawing). The contact screen is ink on the page's own paper (`useInkWriting`: no sheet of its own, so the backdrop's grain and bamboo run on behind it and the page reads as one picture), the location above, the channels as brush icons above the title with the paper plane (the form, ringed with splashed ink, `InkSplash`) beside them: in turn ink drops from an icon, lands with a crown of streaks and droplets, soaks out and runs into the channel's name in the brush hand below the title; the word stays a few seconds (longer, on damp-looking paper, while pointed at) and is that channel's link; pointing at or focusing an icon drips it at once, clicking it opens the channel; the title "Contact me" is the link of the latest drop's channel (falling, writing, written, or the last one between drops; `home.closing.titleLink`). The canvas loads the brush hand itself (`document.fonts.load`), since the Chinese page shows it nowhere else. Every other channel opens in a new tab, like every link to another site (its accessible name says so, `common.newTab`); the CV downloads in place. In both themes the email channel opens the contact form (`ContactDialog`: the existing `ContactForm` in a native modal dialog, mounted on first opening and kept, Lenis stopped and `html:has(dialog.contact-dialog[open])` locking the page), since a mailto link does nothing without a mail app. Every section fades in as it scrolls into view and out as it leaves (`useSectionFades`: opacity only, one page-long trigger; the statement's pin forbids a transform). Page glass (`PageGlass` + `usePageGlass`, `.page-glass` in index.css; the owner's pick of two prototypes): one pane of frosted glass for the whole page (`backdrop-filter` blur, a tint per theme from the `--glass-*` tokens, light enough that the backdrop still shows, a hairline edge and shine, rounded), a portal into <body>, fixed at `z-index: -5` above the backdrop and under the sections, so no section's fading opacity ever becomes its backdrop root. It wraps the content box (`[data-glass-box]`: the text of In brief, the four chapters and the archive) nearest the middle of the screen, clipped under the header and above the screen's foot, and glides and resizes to the next box as the reader moves on (transform plus px size, eased on gsap's ticker only while something changes; reduced motion jumps); the hero and the contact screen have no box, so it fades out over them. While the page moves it fades out (quickly), so the backdrop shows whole, and 220 ms after the last scroll event it fades back in (more slowly) where the reader stopped, so the text reads off a calm surface; with reduced motion it stays. Placed at once on the first frame. Hidden (`visibility`) when no box is in view; lighter blur on phones. Reduced motion: still scenes, the word written at once, no fades. Tags (`TagList`, every list: experience, projects, skills) are links to a web search for the term in the page's language (`tags.searchQuery`: "what is React" / "React 是什麼", `tagSearch.ts`), in a new tab. While a tag is hovered or focused a tiny cartoon cat (`EdgeCat`, SVG, `.edge-cat*` in index.css: transforms and opacity only) runs back and forth along its top edge, a new random colour each time (black, orange, white or rainbow, `cats.ts`); with reduced motion the cat sits still. Project cards (reserved slots too) get a light instead (`.card-light*` in index.css) on hover, focus and while crossing the middle of the screen (IntersectionObserver band): a comet (conic gradient, rotated by a transform) runs round the border without end (clockwise, a lap about 4s, the laps joining seamlessly) masked to a 2px ring, glowing (the same comet through a wider halo with soft-edged masks: no drop-shadow, which WebKit paints on the main thread, stalling the light on phones), cycling through the colours (hue-rotate) with brief flares (brightness) and flickering (opacity dips); every moving part stays on the compositor (static masks, a transform, colour-only filters), and the comet is sized from the card's longer side (container units), just over its diagonal; the filter animation is, like the ink canvas, an exception to "transform and opacity only"; reduced motion: a still comet. A real project with a link is clickable as a whole: the title link stretches over the card (`after:inset-0`, new tab), the tags stay their own links above it (`relative z-10`). The section dock (`SectionDock`, a portal into <body>) floats at the foot of the home page once it has scrolled past 80px: Top and the five nav sections, the current one marked (a darker pill in light mode, `bg-fg/16`), above everything on the page (`z-[60]`), then, once a CV is uploaded, a CV download button (accessible name `content.contact.cvDownload`); below `sm` the section links scroll sideways behind a faded edge and the download stays in view; `inert` while hidden; a CSS fade and rise. The hero shows the same download ("Download CV", `PDF · <size>`) in the cell left of the roles, above the scroll cue (the cue alone is `aria-hidden`). "In brief" has the owner's cats behind it (`.statement-photo` in index.css, `src/assets/statement-cats-{720,1280}.webp`: a monochrome WebP made with Pillow (greyscale, auto-contrast, no metadata), `srcset`, lazy, `alt=""` in an `aria-hidden` layer): two intersected masks (a soft ellipse right of centre and a ramp that keeps it faint where the text runs), at a low opacity over the page backdrop (plain blending: a blend mode would change as the section fades), a `[data-speed]` parallax drift (`SPEED.statementPhoto`) over a 12% overscan; below `md` fainter and faded at the top and bottom. Text keeps over 7:1 contrast on it.
